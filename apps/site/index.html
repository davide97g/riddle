For the reMarkable 2

A diary that writes back.

Write on your reMarkable and pause. Your ink fades, and the diary answers in its own hand.

Three seconds is the whole interface.

  1. You write.

    In ink, on any page of any notebook. A small agent on the tablet reads the pen. Your notebooks, menus and sync stay as they were.

  2. You stop.

    Three seconds without the pen is a question. What you wrote is rubbed off the page with the eraser, the way you would do it yourself.

  3. It answers.

    The reply is written by driving the pen, one stroke at a time, in a hand of its own. A word it leans on comes out heavier.

The rest of it.

The tablet never leaves your network.

Ink only crosses your own Wi-Fi, to a computer you own. The page is the only thing that can be published, and it carries a password. Guests, if you let them in, can only read.

Say it instead.

Speak from your phone and it is transcribed on your own machine, by a speech model that never phones home.

Watch the page.

The tablet's screen, about once a second, read-only. No model involved.

Hand it a pdf.

A pdf, an epub or a picture goes into the tablet's library as a real document you can write on.

It remembers a little.

A handful of lines about you, kept between pages in a plain text file you can read and edit.

On your iPhone and your Mac.

Talk to the diary, type to it, watch the page live and send it documents. The whole conversation, in the order it happened, with the pages you wrote in it.

What you need.

A reMarkable 2
On your Wi-Fi, reachable over ssh as root, which it already allows. Nothing else is installed on it.
A computer that stays on
A Mac or a small Linux box on the same network. It holds the pen and the conversation.
An OpenAI key
For the replies, and for reading a page when you ask. Your own key, your own bill.
An evening
The setup is a handful of commands, and the tablet needs calibrating once.
# the agent, cross-compiled and put on the tablet
./riddle device build

# check everything before trusting it
./riddle doctor

# open a notebook on the tablet, then
./riddle start

Questions.

Does it need a jailbroken tablet?

No. It uses the root ssh access every reMarkable 2 already offers, and installs one small agent. Nothing like Toltec is involved.

Will it write over my notes?

It writes on whatever page is open, which is the point. It only rubs out your question and its own ink. Turn off Vanish and answer and it leaves the page alone entirely.

Where does my handwriting go?

A picture of what you wrote goes to OpenAI to be read, on each turn. The database, the recordings and the pictures themselves stay on your machine. The privacy notes in the repository list every file it keeps.

Can I see one running?

Mine is at riddle.davideghiotto.it. Skip the password and look around as a guest: everything it has written so far, and what it writes next, as it happens. A guest can read and nothing else. Nothing you do there reaches the pen.

Does it work on the Paper Pro or the Move?

It is built and calibrated on a reMarkable 2. The other models are untested, and the pen injection depends on the digitizer, so expect work.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is MIT-licensed. The only running cost is the model, which is billed to your own API key.
